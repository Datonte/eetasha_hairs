Customer care

Returns & Refunds

We want you to love your hair. Because hair is a personal hygiene product, our returns are handled differently from most shops. Please read this before you order.

Every unit is sent with our branded eetashacollection tag, which acts as a hygiene seal. Please keep the tag on until you have checked your order. Once the tag is removed, the hair can't be returned for hygiene reasons.

Wrong or faulty item

If we've sent you the wrong unit, or it arrives faulty or not as described:

  • Please email us as soon as possible, ideally within 48 hours of delivery (or reply to your order email), so we can sort it out quickly.
  • Send clear photos or a video showing the issue, and keep the tag on and the hair unworn if you can.
  • We'll replace or refund the item, including the delivery charge, and cover the return postage.

These are your legal rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. They still apply if you contact us after 48 hours.

Changed your mind? 14 days to cancel

You can cancel your order within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason, as long as the hair is unworn and the tag (hygiene seal) is still attached.

  • Tell us within 14 days: email us, reply to your order email, or use the cancellation form below.
  • Send it back within 14 days of telling us, unworn, with the tag on and in its original packaging, using a tracked service. Return postage is paid by you.
  • Your refund: the price of the item and the standard delivery charge you paid (if you chose a faster delivery, we refund the cost of our standard option). We refund within 14 days of receiving the item back, or of you showing us proof you've sent it, whichever is sooner, to the same payment method.
  • If the item comes back used or damaged, we may reduce the refund to cover its loss in value.

When the 14 days to cancel don't apply

  • Hygiene seal removed: for hygiene reasons, hair can't be returned once the tag (hygiene seal) has been removed, or if it has been worn, washed, cut, coloured, bleached, plucked or styled, or the lace has been cut.
  • Custom units: wigs made, coloured or styled to your order are made to your specification, so they can't be returned if you change your mind.

Normal shedding or tangling from wear and styling, and slight natural differences in colour and texture, are not faults.

Delivery

  • UK: tracked delivery, with free tracked delivery on orders over £400. Next-day delivery is also available.
  • International (Europe, USA & Canada, Africa and worldwide): tracked express delivery.
  • In-stock items are usually dispatched within 5–10 working days, including wig-making. Items marked "out of stock – coming back soon" can't be ordered until they're back.
  • International orders: import taxes, duties and customs fees in your country are not included and are paid by you on delivery.

International orders

This policy applies to all orders. Import taxes, duties and customs fees charged in your country can't be refunded by us.

Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights as a consumer.
