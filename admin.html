Pay later (Klarna & Clearpay)

Offer "Pay in 3 interest-free instalments"
First switch on Klarna in your Stripe dashboard (Settings → Payment methods → Klarna). You are paid in full straight away; the customer pays Klarna back in instalments. Until Klarna is on in Stripe, customers simply pay by card.
Offer Clearpay "Pay in 4" (UK orders only)
First switch on Clearpay in your Stripe dashboard (Settings → Payment methods → Afterpay / Clearpay). The customer pays in 4 parts over 6 weeks; you are paid in full straight away. It shows on UK orders whose address includes a postcode. Clearpay has a maximum order size, so very large orders get card (or Klarna) instead.
