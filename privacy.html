Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information eetashacollection collects when you visit our website or buy from us, why we need it, who we share it with and the choices you have. We keep it to what we need to get your hair to you.

In short: we use your details to take payment, deliver your order and help you if you contact us. We never see your card details, we don't sell your information, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who we are

eetashacollection (eetashacollection.com) is an online shop run by a sole trader in England. We are the "controller" of the personal information described here, which means we decide how it's used and we're responsible for looking after it.

Questions about your information? Email eetasha.collection@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, the items you bought, the delivery option you chose and the order total.
  • Payment: you pay on Stripe's secure payment page, so your card details go straight to Stripe (or to Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna, Clearpay, Revolut or Amazon if you choose them). We only receive confirmation that you've paid, the payment method type and the amount.
  • If you create an account: your name and email address. Your password is stored in encrypted form by our secure login provider; we can't see it.
  • If you contact us: what you send us by email or Instagram, such as questions, photos or videos of an order.
  • Technical information: like every website, our hosting provider receives your IP address and browser details when you visit, which are used to run and protect the site.

Why we use it (and our legal reasons)

  • To take and deliver your order, send your receipt and delivery updates, and handle returns or refunds. This is needed to carry out our contract with you.
  • To keep business and tax records as the law requires. This is a legal obligation.
  • To answer your questions, prevent fraud and keep the website and your account secure. These are our legitimate interests, and we only use what's needed for them.

We don't send marketing emails. If we ever want to, we'll ask for your permission first and you can say no or unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

We only share what each service needs to do its job. These companies act on our instructions and must keep your information safe:

  • Stripe processes payments. If you choose Klarna or Clearpay, they receive the details they need to offer you instalments, under their own privacy policies.
  • Delivery companies (such as Royal Mail, DPD, Evri, DHL, UPS or FedEx) receive your name, address and phone number so they can deliver your parcel and contact you about it.
  • Supabase stores our product, order and account database and handles secure sign-in.
  • Vercel hosts our website.
  • Resend sends our order emails (receipts and delivery updates).
  • Google provides the email account we reply from, and the fonts on our website (your browser fetches them from Google, which shares your IP address with Google).

We never sell your personal information. We may share it if the law requires us to, for example with HMRC or the police.

Information stored outside the UK

Some of the services above store information in the European Economic Area or the USA. When that happens, your information is protected by safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK–US Data Bridge or the International Data Transfer Agreement and Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Orders and receipts: 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as required for tax records.
  • Your account: until you ask us to delete it. Orders you placed are still kept as business records as above.
  • Emails and messages: for as long as we need them to help you, and no longer than 2 years after your last order or message, unless they relate to an order.

Cookies and browser storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't use analytics that follow you around the web. We only use storage that the website needs to work:

  • Your bag: kept in your browser so it's still there when you come back.
  • Checkout: your delivery choice and the details you've typed are kept in your browser until you close the tab, so you don't lose them if you go back a page.
  • Sign-in: if you log in to your account, a secure login token is kept in your browser.
  • Payment page: Stripe uses its own cookies on its payment page to process payments and prevent fraud.

Because these are strictly necessary for the site to work, we don't need to ask for your consent to them. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that's wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information (we may need to keep order records for tax reasons);
  • ask us to limit how we use your information, or object to how we use it;
  • ask for your information in a format you can take to another business.

To use any of these rights, email eetasha.collection@gmail.com. It's free, and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Keeping your information safe

Our website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), payments are handled by Stripe, access to order information is limited to us, and our accounts are protected with strong passwords. No website can be 100% secure, but we take care to protect your information.

Children

Our website is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
